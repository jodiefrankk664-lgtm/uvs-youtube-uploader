Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how UVS YouTube Uploader ("the app"), operated by the owner of the Ungal Veetu Seithi YouTube channel (brand account svunnam, @ungalveetuseithi), accesses, uses, stores and shares Google user data.

1. Who uses the app

The app is a private publishing tool. Its only user is the owner of the Ungal Veetu Seithi channel. It is not offered to the public.

2. Google user data the app accesses

With the channel owner's consent through Google OAuth 2.0, the app requests these YouTube Data API scopes: youtube.upload (upload videos), youtube (manage the channel's videos, playlists and thumbnails) and youtube.force-ssl (manage captions). Through them the app accesses the authorized channel's basic channel information (channel ID and title) and the videos, metadata, thumbnails, playlists and captions that the owner uploads or edits with the app.

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the channel owner's own videos to the owner's own channel and to manage their metadata, thumbnails, playlists, captions and privacy status. The data is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics resale or any other purpose.

4. Storage and security

OAuth credentials (access token and refresh token) are stored only on the channel owner's own computer or server, in access-restricted files, and are sent only to Google's OAuth and YouTube API endpoints over HTTPS. The app does not keep a copy of any YouTube data on third-party servers. Video files and metadata stay on the owner's systems and on YouTube.

5. Sharing

The app does not sell, rent, transfer or share Google user data with any third party. Data is sent only to Google/YouTube in order to perform the actions the owner requests.

6. Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the owner uses the app. The owner can delete the stored token files at any time and can revoke the app's access at Google Account → Third-party apps & services. After revocation, the app can no longer access the channel.

8. YouTube and Google policies

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

9. Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact
