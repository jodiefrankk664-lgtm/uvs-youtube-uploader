UVS YouTube Uploader

Upload tool for the Ungal Veetu Seithi YouTube channel (brand account: svunnam, handle: @ungalveetuseithi).

What this app does

UVS YouTube Uploader is an internal publishing tool operated by the owner of the Ungal Veetu Seithi YouTube channel. It uses the YouTube Data API v3 to upload the channel's own videos and to set their title, description, tags, thumbnail, playlist and privacy status.

The tool acts only on the channel that authorized it. It does not access, collect or modify data from any other YouTube channel or Google account, and it is not offered to the public.

Google sign-in and permissions

The channel owner signs in once with Google OAuth 2.0 and grants the app permission to manage videos on the channel. The app requests only the YouTube scopes needed to upload and manage the channel's videos.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using this app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
